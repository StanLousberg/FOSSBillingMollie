## Round Mollie icon

`module/Molliepayments/assets/mollie-icon.png` is the **unaltered circular Mollie icon** from the [official Mollie PrestaShop integration](https://github.com/mollie/PrestaShop/blob/master/views/img/mollie_panel_icon.png), maintained by Mollie. The original source file is named `views/img/mollie_panel_icon.png` and its original resolution is 32 × 32 pixels.

The icon is used to identify the supported payment provider on GitHub and in the FOSSBilling extension manifest. It is not an original project logo, nor does its inclusion imply endorsement, affiliation or sponsorship by Mollie B.V. or FOSSBilling.

For other formats and official usage guidance, consult [Mollie's resources page](https://www.mollie.com/resources). Do not alter, recolor, reshape or combine Mollie's original brand asset with other elements.
